Made to be finished

Four totals in. One useful answer out.

The proposed product is deliberately smaller than a bookkeeping system. It is a monthly check-in, not a new habit to maintain every day.

01

Take the month's totals

Use the booking revenue, platform fees and cleaning totals you already have. No account connection or CSV setup in this preview.

02

Add running costs

Enter the other costs you want counted, such as supplies, utilities or repairs. Skip categories that don't apply.

03

See the simple result

Compare listed costs with revenue and estimate what's left. No tax, mortgage or profitability promises hidden in the calculation.

Two ways to use the same check

Pick the format that feels easiest.

Browser mini-tool

Open a page and do the check. No installation or sign-in. The planned version will keep the figures on your device.
